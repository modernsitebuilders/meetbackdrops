## After generation: you pick the categories

1. Cull to keepers and drop the raw PNGs in `~/Downloads`.
2. Number + dry-run classify (no upload): `process_new_images.py --number`, then `--classify-only` in chunks.
3. Load the batch into the review tool (`image-pipeline/review-tool`, launch config `review-tool`, localhost:8842) with local previews, and pick each image's category.
